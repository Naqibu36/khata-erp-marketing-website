# Khata ERP - Professional Business Management Software

**Live Website:** [https://khata-erp-marketing-website.vercel.app/](https://khata-erp-marketing-website.vercel.app/)

**Khata ERP** is a modern, all-in-one business management and ERP solution designed for retail stores, pharmacies, laboratories, wholesalers, and growing businesses. It simplifies daily operations, financial tracking, and online order management in one unified platform.

## Key Features

- **Point of Sale (POS):** Fast billing, barcode scanning, and instant receipt generation for retail and wholesale counters.
- **Inventory Management:** Real-time stock tracking, low-stock alerts, batch management, and supplier records.
- **Pharmacy & Laboratory Management:** Specialized modules for medicine expiry tracking, lab test reporting, and patient records.
- **Complete Accounting & Khata:** Automated ledger entries, expense tracking, profit and loss reports, and customer/supplier balances.
- **Online Orders Integration:** Manage digital orders seamlessly alongside physical store sales.
